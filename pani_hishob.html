Method

  • Crop water productivity = yield (kg/ha) ÷ water (m³/ha). 1 mm of water over 1 ha is 10 m³.
  • Satellite ET is the field's SEBAL actual ET for the season the crop was sown in (Kharif sown Jun–Sep, Rabi Oct–Jan, Zaid Feb–May), from the Datli–Musalgaon field app. When two crops on one Gat fall in the same season, the season total is split between them by their Kc × ETo need. Kharif 2025 rests on few cloud-free Landsat scenes.
  • Crop need = Σ Kc × ETo, day by day from the reported sowing to harvest. Kc curves come from the Kc app's Maharashtra university tables (MPKV Rahuri), stretched to the farmer's own season length. The HS column is used where the table has one, to match the HS ETo. Carrot, coriander, dill and lucerne have no university table and use FAO-56 values.
  • Irrigation given = number of irrigations × depth per irrigation. The survey records only the count, so the depth is an assumption. Rain is not counted here, so Kharif values run high.
  • Sowing and harvest are months in the survey, set to the 15th (±15 days). The NDVI chart shows Sentinel-2 clear-sky images for the Gat with the satellite's green-up to harvest season from the farmer-vs-satellite check.
  • Village medians use Gats farmed whole (40 of 49) with a reported yield. Gats farmed only in part are drawn hollow. Flags from the survey cleaning are shown on each crop so they can be rechecked with the farmer.
