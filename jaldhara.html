Step 1 · पायरी १

Where is the field?शेत कुठे आहे?

Click the map, paste coordinates, or pick a station. Latitude and the day of the year alone fix the solar radiation — that part needs no sensor at all.

Why the map matters. How strong the sun is over your field today depends on just two things: how far north you are, and which day of the year it is. That is pure geometry, so the app can compute it exactly — no instrument needed. Elevation matters a little too, because thinner air changes the air pressure in the equations.

नकाशा का? — सूर्याची ताकद फक्त अक्षांश आणि वर्षातील दिवस यावर ठरते. ही निव्वळ भूमिती असल्याने कोणत्याही यंत्राशिवाय अचूक मोजता येते. उंचीचा थोडा परिणाम हवेच्या दाबामुळे होतो.

Map tiles need an internet connection. Everything else still works — set the coordinates by hand.

Step 2 · पायरी २

What was the weather?त्या दिवशीचे हवामान

Temperature alone is enough for Hargreaves–Samani. Add humidity and wind and the full Penman–Monteith standard unlocks. Have a Class-A pan instead? Fill in the pan block.

Why these numbers. A wide gap between the day's maximum and minimum temperature usually means clear skies, so the gap itself is a clue to how much sun reached the ground. Dry air pulls more water out; wind carries the moist air away and lets more leave. So: temperature is the minimum, humidity and wind make the answer sharper. Leave a box empty and the app says which sensible assumption it used instead.

हे आकडे का? — कमाल व किमान तापमानातील मोठा फरक म्हणजे निरभ्र आकाश, म्हणजेच जास्त सूर्यप्रकाश. कोरडी हवा जास्त पाणी ओढते, वारा ओलसर हवा दूर नेतो. तापमान किमान गरज; आर्द्रता व वारा असल्यास उत्तर अधिक अचूक.

Step 3 · पायरी ३

Reference ET, three waysसंदर्भीय बाष्पोत्सर्जन — तीन पद्धतींनी

The same day, the same site, computed by each method. Differences here are exactly why a Kc column must be paired with the ETo method it was derived against.

What ETo means. Picture a well-watered patch of short grass next to your field. The water it would lose today, in millimetres of depth, is the reference — ETo. It is the thirst of the weather itself, before any crop is considered. One millimetre is 10,000 litres over a hectare. Penman–Monteith is the international standard and needs the full weather record. Pan evaporation reads water loss from a real pan and corrects it with a pan coefficient. Hargreaves–Samani needs only a thermometer, and runs a little high in humid weather unless its coefficient is calibrated locally.

ETo म्हणजे काय? — भरपूर पाणी दिलेल्या गवताच्या संदर्भ क्षेत्रातून एका दिवसात उडणारे पाणी (मिमी). हे हवामानाची तहान — पीक विचारात घेण्याआधीची. १ मिमी = प्रति हेक्टर १०,००० लिटर.

Step 4 · पायरी ४

Which crop, which week?कोणते पीक, कोणता आठवडा?

Crop coefficients are read from Crop Coefficients for Efficient Irrigation Management (AICRP-IWM, VNMKV Parbhani, 2026), digitised page by page from all three Maharashtra agricultural universities.

What Kc does. Your crop is not grass. A crop coefficient is the number that converts the grass reference into this crop at this stage: ETc = Kc × ETo. Just after sowing the soil is bare and Kc is small; at full canopy it can pass 1.0, because a dense green crop loses more than short grass; near harvest it falls again. One caution the app enforces for you — a Kc measured against Penman–Monteith belongs with a Penman–Monteith ETo, never with another method's.

Kc म्हणजे काय? — गवताच्या संदर्भाचे तुमच्या पिकात व अवस्थेत रूपांतर करणारा गुणांक: ETc = Kc × ETo. पेरणीनंतर लहान, पूर्ण वाढीत १ पेक्षाही जास्त, काढणीजवळ पुन्हा कमी.

Step 5 · पायरी ५

The season planहंगामाचा आराखडा

From millimetres to litres. Three simple steps. Take away the rain that actually soaks in and stays for the crop — effective rain, not every drop that falls. Divide by how much of the pumped water really reaches the roots: flood is wasteful, drip is not. Then multiply by your area — a hectare, an acre or a guntha — and the answer comes out in litres and cubic metres, the units a pump and a farm pond are measured in.

मिमी वरून लिटरकडे — प्रभावी पाऊस वजा करा, सिंचन कार्यक्षमतेने भागा, आणि क्षेत्राने गुणा. उत्तर लिटर व घनमीटरमध्ये — पंप आणि शेततळ्याच्या भाषेत.
